## 📊 Dashboard Pages

Each dashboard has **3 pages**:

| Page | Focus |
|------|-------|
| **Page 1: Demographics** | Who is watching? (Gender, Age, Province, Race) |
| **Page 2: Time Trends** | When are they watching? (Peak Hours, Day of Week, Monthly Trend) |
| **Page 3: Content & Channels** | What are they watching? (Top Channels, Province Consumption) |
